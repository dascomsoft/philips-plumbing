About Philip's Plumbing

OUR STORY

From Small Repairs to Joburg's Trusted Plumbers

Philip's Plumbing started in 2013 as a one-man operation with a simple mission: provide honest, reliable plumbing services at fair prices. Philip, a qualified plumber with years of experience, built the business on word-of-mouth and customer satisfaction.

Today, we've grown into a team of 8 certified plumbers, but we've kept our family-owned values. We're still the same company that answers the phone at 2am when your geyser bursts, and still the same company that takes time to explain the problem and your options.

We're proud to be PIRB-registered, fully insured, and trusted by hundreds of homeowners and businesses across Johannesburg.
